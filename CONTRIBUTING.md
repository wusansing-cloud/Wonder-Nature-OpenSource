# Contributing to Wonder Nature · 贡献指南

Wonder Nature 以 MIT 协议开放源码。欢迎你 Fork 后自由修改、学习与二次开发，也欢迎通过 Issues 反馈问题、提出建议、交流想法。

*Wonder Nature is MIT-licensed. Fork it freely to study, modify and build your own thing. Issues are welcome for bug reports, ideas and discussion.*

## 关于官方仓库 · About the official repository

- 官方仓库的代码由作者维护，**不合并外部代码到官方分支**。你的二改归你的二改，官方是官方，二者不混用。
- 官方网站 `https://wonder-nature.xingh.cc` 与官方发布版本始终由作者控制。
- 如果你希望基于本项目做一个衍生版，请 Fork 后在自己的仓库里开发；衍生版本请明确与官方项目区分，不要冒充官方 Wonder Nature。

*The official repository is maintained by the author and does not merge outside code into the official branch — your fork is your fork, the official project stays the official project. The official site and releases remain under the author's control. If you build a derivative, develop it in your own fork and keep it clearly distinct from the official Wonder Nature.*

## 反馈问题 · Reporting a bug

请在 Issue 中说明：

- 你期望发生什么，实际发生了什么；
- 浏览器与系统（以及是否开启硬件加速 / WebGL——关闭时场景会回退到 Canvas 软件渲染）；
- 复现步骤，视觉问题请附截图；
- 页脚显示的版本号（当前为 `v0.2.0`）。

*In an issue, include what you expected vs what happened, browser and OS (and whether WebGL is enabled — the scene falls back to a Canvas software renderer when it isn't), steps to reproduce, a screenshot for visual problems, and the version shown in the footer (currently `v0.2.0`).*

## 提交建议 · Suggesting ideas

对自然科普、交互设计、WebGL、3D 可视化、创意编程有想法的开发者，欢迎在 Issues 中交流。好的建议可能会被作者吸收进未来的官方版本。

*Ideas about natural science, interaction design, WebGL, 3D visualization and creative coding are welcome in Issues. Good suggestions may find their way into a future official release.*

## 行为准则 · Code of conduct

对事不对人。对作品的批评没问题，人身攻击不行。

*Be civil and specific. Criticism of the work is fine; personal attacks are not.*
